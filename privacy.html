Privacy

What the website huinsight.com collects about its visitors, and what it doesn’t.

Who runs this site

Huinsight 慧眼 is an open-source project by Ray. Contact: hello@huinsight.com.

What the site collects

No cookies and no analytics. The page loads nothing from other sites.

Your browser stores your language (hs-lang) locally; it never leaves your device.

The host, GitHub Pages, keeps server logs such as IP addresses. We never see them (GitHub’s statement).

The app is separate

The Huinsight app runs on your own machine or cloud account and sends us nothing. Its AI features talk only to the model provider you choose, with your own key.

Your rights

We hold no data about visitors. If you email us, we keep the message only to answer it, and erase it when you ask: hello@huinsight.com.

本站收集什么
